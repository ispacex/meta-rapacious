From now on we restrict ouself to the computation of 3-primary unstable groups mod A, and use the notation $$\pi^n_i={}_3\pi_i(S^n)/A^n_i,\qquad Q^n_i={}_3\pi_i(Q^n_2)/AQ^n_i\quad\textit{and}\quad \pi^S_k={}_3\pi^S_k/A^S_k$$ for the sake of simplicity.

The computation of the 3-primary components of the $k$-stem groups $\pi^n_{n+k}$ for odd $n$ will be done usually by induction on $k$ and $n$ for the following sequence of double suspensisons $E^2$

(5.1.2) $$\pi^3_{3+k}\xrightarrow{E^2}\pi^5_{5+k}\xrightarrow{E^2}\cdots\xrightarrow{E^2}\pi^{2m-1}_{2m-1+k}\xrightarrow{E^2}\pi^{2m+1}_{2m+1+k}\xrightarrow{E^2}\cdots\xrightarrow{S^\infty}\pi^s_k,$$

where we assume that the stable group $\pi^S_k$ and the unstable groups $\pi^{2m+1}_{2m+1+j}$ for $j<k$ are already fixed.

Each double suspension $E^2$ is verified by using EHP-sequence (2.1.1) of $p=3$ which is rewritten as the following exact sequence :

(5.1.3) $$\cdots\xrightarrow{H}Q^{2m-1}_{2m-1+k}\xrightarrow{P}\pi^{2m-1}_{2m-1+k}\xrightarrow{E^2}\pi^{2m+1}_{2m+1+k}\xrightarrow{H}Q^{2m-1}_{2m-2+k}\xrightarrow{P}\cdots.$$
