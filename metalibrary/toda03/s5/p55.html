Lemma 5.2 Assume that Hopf invariant $H(\xi)$ of an element $\xi\in\pi_{i+4}(S^{2p+1})$ is M-represented by $\gamma$ for $\gamma\in\pi_{i+1}(Y^{2p^2-2})$. Then up to non-zero coefficient there holds $$JH(\partial_\alpha(\xi))=H_p(\partial\alpha(\xi))\,\text{[sic]}=\overline\beta_1\circ E^2\gamma\,,$$ where $\overline\beta_1:Y^{2p^2}\to S^{2p+1}$ is an extension of $\beta_1(2p+1)$.

Proof. $\partial_\alpha$ is induced by $d_\alpha:\Omega S^{2p+1}\to S^3$ and $H_p$ is induced by $h_p:\Omega S^3\to\Omega S^{2p+1}$. Then $H_p\partial_\alpha$ is induced by $h_p\circ\Omega d_\alpha:\Omega^2S^{2p+1}\to\Omega S^{2p+1}$, whose restriction on $S^{2p-1}$ is homotopic to zero. Thus $h_p\circ\Omega d_\alpha$ induces a map $$h':Q^{2p-1}_2=\Omega(\Omega^2S^{2p+1},S^{2p-1})\longrightarrow\Omega^2S^{2p+1}\,.$$ Let $\overline\beta_1:Y^{2p^2}\to S^{2p+1}$ be adjoint to $h'\circ g_{2p-1}:Y^{2p^2-2}\to Q^{2p-1}_2\to\Omega^2S^{2p+1}$, then Theorem 3.5 shows that $\overline\beta_1|S^{2p^2-1}=\beta_1(2p+1)$. Then the lemma follows. $\square$
