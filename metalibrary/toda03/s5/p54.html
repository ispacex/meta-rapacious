(3) Remaining part

Proof. (1) From the relations $d_1(b^2)=ab^2$ and $d_1(B^2)=AB^2$ in (1) of Theorem 5.2, we have $d_1(b^3)=ab^3$ and $d_1(B^3)=AB^3$ by composing $\beta_1$ from the right. The remaining relations on $d_1$ follow from Proposition 5.1.

(2) The short range unstable elements in $k=36,43,45$ are established by Propositions 5.2,5.3. The removability of the collections in $k=43,45$ follows from Lemma 3.7,(1) and one in $k=36$ from Lemma 3.7,(2).

In $k=40$, $d_1(AB_2)=d_1(A_1)\circ\beta_2=a_2\circ\beta_2$ is represented by $\alpha_2\beta_2=-\{\alpha_1,\alpha_1,3\}\beta_2=\alpha_1\{\alpha_1,3,\beta_2\}=\alpha_1\beta_1^3$ in $S^9$. Thus $d_1(AB_2)=ab^3$. The other 3 simple unstable elements in $k=40,41$ are established by Proposition 5.1 and naturality. The removability follows from Lemma 2.4.
