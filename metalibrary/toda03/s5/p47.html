5.2 Computation diagram mod A of lower stems

[p. 47]

In order to compute groups $\pi^n_{n+k}$ succesively, we use computing diagrams mod A, omitting the symbols $E^2$, $H$ and $P$, replacing $Q^n_{n+k}$ by its generators. We also replace $\pi^n_{n+k}$ by a collection of symbols which indicate direct summands such as

(5.2.1) $$\bullet\cong\mathbf Z/3,\qquad\circ\cong\mathbf Z/9\quad\textit{and}\quad\triangleright\cong\mathbf Z/27.$$

The symbol $=$ at $E^2$ means bijective such as

(5.2.2) $$\bullet=\bullet,\qquad\circ=\circ\qquad\textit{and}\qquad\triangleright=\triangleright.$$

The synbol $\to$ at $E^2$ means not bijective and non-trivial such as

(5.2.3) $$\bullet\to\circ,\quad\circ\to\triangleright,\quad\circ\to\bullet,\quad\triangleright\to\circ\quad\circ\to\circ\quad\textit{and}\quad\triangleright\to\triangleright,$$

where the first two are injective, the next two are surjective and the last two are $p$ times.

First observe the 10-stem groups ${}_3\pi_{n+10}(S^n)$ : $${}_3\pi_{13}(S^3)\xrightarrow{E^2}{}_3\pi_{15}(S^5)\xrightarrow{E^2}{}_3\pi_{17}(S^7).$$ This is represented as $$\bullet\longrightarrow\circ\longrightarrow\bullet\,.$$ The equality $\Delta\circ E^2=p\cdot$ of Lemma 2.1 implies that $$Q^1_{13}=\langle b\rangle\cong\mathbf Z/3\quad\textit{and}\quad Q^1_{14}=0\,.$$ This is the only case we know that (5.1.5) cannot be applied.
